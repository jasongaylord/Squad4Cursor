# Ceremonies

Lightweight sprint rituals the team can run on request.

| Ceremony | Cadence | Owner | Purpose |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Standup | on request | Lead | Surface blockers and focus |
| Retro | end of feature | Lead + Scribe | Capture wisdom into identity/ |
| Decision review | weekly or on request | Lead | Prune/stale-check decisions.md |
| Nap | when context grows large | Scribe / human via `s4c nap` (Phase B) | Compress histories |
